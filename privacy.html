Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

The Spot (findthespot.net) is a map of Bay Area events. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect only what the site needs to work, we don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

What we collect

If you browse the map

You don't need an account to browse. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. To keep the site secure and working, our servers briefly process your IP address (for example, to stop spam by limiting how many requests come from one network). See "Services we use" for the companies that help run the site.

If you create an account

  • Username — shown to you in the site's menu.
  • Email address — used to confirm your account, reset your password, and contact you about your account. We don't send marketing email.
  • Password — stored only as a one-way scrambled "hash." Nobody at The Spot can see your actual password.
  • Login token — saved in your browser so you stay logged in for up to 7 days. You can end it anytime by logging out, or on every device from your Account page.

Events you post and reports you make

Events you post (name, venue, address, date, time, link, age restriction) are public on the map. They're linked to your account so you can edit or delete them, but your username and email are not shown with them. If you report an event, we record that your account reported it so each person can only report an event once; reports aren't shown publicly.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for or access your device's location, camera, microphone, contacts, or payment details.

How we use it

  • To run the map, your account, and the events you post
  • To keep the site safe — preventing spam, abuse, and fake accounts
  • To send account emails you need (email confirmation, password resets)
  • To fix problems — error logs help us find bugs

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Services we use

These companies process some data on our behalf to run The Spot:

  • Cloudflare — hosts the website, protects it from attacks, and provides privacy-friendly visit statistics that don't use cookies.
  • Railway — runs our servers and stores our database.
  • Google Maps — shows the map and finds addresses. Google receives your IP address and how you use the map, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Ticketmaster — supplies many of the event listings. "View Tickets" links take you to Ticketmaster, where their own privacy policy applies.
  • Resend — sends our account emails.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information until you delete your account. Events disappear from the map once their date has passed. Rate-limiting information about IP addresses is held only temporarily. Our hosting providers keep server logs for a limited time.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account — anytime from your Account page. This permanently deletes your account, your events, and your reports.
  • Edit or delete your events — from My Events.
  • Access or correct your data — email us and we'll help.

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request a copy, to have it deleted or corrected, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising. To make a request, email us at the address below.

Children

The Spot is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it. Note that many listed events are 18+ or 21+.

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, rate limits against password guessing, and security headers. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects your information, we'll let you know.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let account holders know.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@findthespot.net
